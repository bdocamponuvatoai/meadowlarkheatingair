## Enquiry form

The form state lives only in the browser. Visitors can review their enquiry and download it as a text file. **No API receives entries, no email is sent and no visit is scheduled.** The contact page, the form notice, FAQ 5 and the privacy page all say so, and that wording should stay until a verified contact channel and a business-approved privacy policy are in place.
